# Findservice
Findservice is a local service marketplace platform designed to help small businesses and service providers grow their businesses and improve their accessibility to customers through a simple and user-friendly digital platform. The platform connects customers with trusted professionals across multiple service categories while allowing service providers to create business profiles, list their services, manage their online presence, and reach a wider audience. Customers can easily search, browse, and connect with verified providers for services such as plumbing, carpentry, photography, cleaning, travel, electrical work, tutoring, and more. ServiceFind focuses on increasing visibility for small businesses, simplifying service discovery, and creating a reliable and convenient environment where customers and service providers can interact efficiently.
Features:
-User Authentication (Login & Signup)
-Customer and Service Provider Accounts
-Service Provider Profiles
-Business Listings Management
-Multiple Service Categories
-Search and Filter Services
-Verified Service Providers
-Ratings and Reviews
-Direct Customer-Provider Contact
-Responsive and User-Friendly Interface
-Profile and Service Management
-Business Visibility and Promotion
-Easy Navigation and Accessibility
-Local Service Discovery
-Secure Data Management
-Mobile-Friendly Design
